## 🔬 About

Researcher at the [UofT Acceleration Consortium](https://acceleration.utoronto.ca/people/yang-bai) working at the intersection of **machine learning and materials science**. I build end-to-end systems — from self-driving lab orchestration and robotic workflows, to ML pipelines for property prediction and reproducible research tooling.

Currently focused on: autonomous catalyst discovery, representation learning for inorganic materials, and AI-assisted scientific workflows.
